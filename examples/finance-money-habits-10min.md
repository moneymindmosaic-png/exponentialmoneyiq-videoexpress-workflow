# Example: The 7 Money Habits Quietly Separating the Rich From Everyone Else

## Production brief

- Length: 10 minutes, target 9:45–10:15 after measuring the completed timeline.
- Frame: Landscape 16:9.
- Style: 2D stickman, consistent Daniel master, clean ink outlines and cel colours.
- Narrator: Christopher, system voice.
- Mood: thoughtful, clear, practical, and respectful.
- Audience: general viewers. Explain habits as useful practices, not a guarantee of wealth.

## Daniel continuity

Reuse the same saved Daniel master throughout this episode and later Exponential Money IQ finance episodes. Daniel has a white circular head, expressive black eyes and eyebrows, charcoal-blue sweater, teal trousers, and dark shoes. Preserve his face, proportions, outfit, colours, and line style in every image and clip. Use the same master as Reference Photo 1. Use a fixed home-office or desk environment as Reference Photo 2 when available. Keep its desk, window, plant, lamp, and camera side in the same positions whenever the scene returns there.

## Script and audio plan

Write about 1,300–1,400 spoken words. Use a natural pace and create the narration with VideoExpress's built-in Text to Speech panel, keeping each segment within the character limit shown in the interface. Use Christopher consistently. Record each segment's measured duration and place it in order on the audio track. Avoid long pauses, repeated lines, and unnatural slowing. Revise the script if the measured narration misses the requested runtime.

| Time target | Section | Main point and visual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:35 | Hook | Daniel checks his payslip and wonders why little remains at month end. Establish that habits can help, while circumstances also matter. |
| 0:35–1:20 | Framing | Introduce seven repeatable choices. Show a simple monthly plan, not a pile of cash or a promise of instant wealth. |

The timestamps are a planning guide. Use actual voice duration and timeline measurements for the final edit. If a section runs long, trim or rewrite its narration while preserving meaning.

## Visual sequence and continuity

Build several distinct visual beats within each chapter. Show Daniel doing the action, then a brief close-up of a relevant prop such as a budget page, emergency repair bill, debt list, training notes, or spending plan. Keep all human figures in the same 2D stickman style. Avoid tiny text inside generated images. Add headings or numbers as editor text where available. Use simple motion or varied compositions to keep the long narration visually active, while preserving Daniel's master design and fixed set geography.

For each scene, record the narration segment, incoming pose, one new action, outgoing pose, props and their positions, asset identity, and timeline placement. A later scene must continue the planned state rather than resetting Daniel to the same pose. Keep generated visual durations aligned with the speech. Do not extend a short clip to cover a long passage unless the editor visibly supports a suitable hold or animation and the result is checked.

## Finance language

Do not claim that wealthy people all follow the same habits or that money struggles come from poor discipline. Note that income, opportunity, health, family needs, and economic conditions shape what is possible. Explain risks plainly. Do not give personal investment, tax, or debt advice. When specific facts are included, verify them against current official sources and list those sources in the video description.
